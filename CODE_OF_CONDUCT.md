# 📜 行为准则 · Code of Conduct

> 中文 | English

## 我们的承诺 · Our Pledge

为了营造一个开放、友善、包容的社区，我们作为成员、贡献者和维护者承诺：不论年龄、体型、残障、种族、性别认同与表达、经验水平、国籍、外貌、宗教或性取向，都让每个人在参与本项目时免于骚扰。We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, religion, or sexual orientation.

## 我们的标准 · Our Standards

### ✅ 鼓励的行为 · Encouraged

- 使用友善、包容的语言。Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- 尊重不同的观点与经验。Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- 优雅地接受建设性批评。Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- 关注于对社区最有益的事。Focusing on what is best for the community.

### ❌ 不可接受的行为 · Unacceptable

- 性化的语言或图像，以及不受欢迎的性关注。Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention.
- 挑衅、侮辱、人身攻击或政治攻击。Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks.
- 公开或私下的骚扰。Public or private harassment.
- 未经明确许可发布他人隐私信息。Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.

## 我们的责任 · Our Responsibilities

项目维护者有责任澄清可接受行为的标准，并对任何不可接受的行为采取适当、公正的纠正措施。Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and taking appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any unacceptable behavior.

## 适用范围 · Scope

本行为准则适用于所有项目空间（Issue、Pull Request、讨论区等）以及个人代表项目或其社区的公共场合。This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community.

## 执行 · Enforcement

如遇辱骂、骚扰或其他不可接受的行为，请通过 issue 或邮件联系维护者。所有投诉都将被保密地审查与调查。Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated confidentially.
